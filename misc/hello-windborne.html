1 - Semantle Strategy

Semantle is a game where you try to guess a single secret word. Every time you enter a guess, you receive a similarity score that tells you how close that word is in meaning to the secret word. Put plainly, the higher the score, the closer the vibes are to the secret word. Below is a strategy to guess the word quickly.

  1. Start with a very large set of base words. Guess these words regardless of their scores. We want a huge variety that includes multiple subjects and types of words (nouns, verbs, and adjectives). Specifically, I always guess the following:
    • Food
    • Sport
    • Feeling
    • Landscape
    • School of thought (-ology)
    • Activity/hobby (verb!)
    • School subject
    • Color
    • Animal
    • Body part
    • Form of art (verb!)
  2. Collect the top score out of your base guesses. Hopefully at least one of these is around a 20 similarity score (if not, consider using a hint). Focus only on that top score and list at least 5 words that you associate with it. Remember to:
    1. Purposefully stray off in different directions here. Likely, the words aren’t very close to the answer yet, so distant associations are welcome.
    2. To help think of new words, I try to change the word parts of speech. Think nouns to adjectives to verbs (e.g. fish to fishy to fishing).
  3. If you get a higher score in step 2, then pick the new highest similarity word and repeat step 2 . If there’s no higher word, go to your second highest base word and repeat step 2.
  4. Once you get a very high similarity score and are close, try making small changes like singular vs. plural or verb tenses. The small changes can help you finish. If you completely run out of ideas, look up your closest guess on a thesaurus. That will ruin the game, but I'm sure you'll get it.


2 - Hobby

A hobby I love is snow skiing. Since I’m usually working indoors, skiing is my excuse in the winter to spend time outside. I love all the obvious things about it. Going fast, carving, jumping, faceshots. But it’s also a great excuse for my friends and me to get together. It’s not something you do in your backyard. You make an event of it and go up to the mountains. The snow provides you with some cushion, so you have the freedom to push your limits and take some risk. The sport itself is combined with beautiful views, drinks at the lodge, and time on chairlifts just talking. You get to nerd out on stylish gear and clothes.

As my friend once put it, there’s something beautiful about ‘controlled falling’ down a hill on two planks of wood!
