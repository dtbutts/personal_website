I Don't Know What's Going On

I Don't Know What's Going On is a blog I started to practice writing. While my career so far has been in engineering and tech, I'm no stranger to the power of good writing. This project helps me develop that skill and keeps me informed on current events.


Visit the site.


On this blog, some friends and I look at the top Google searches of the day, pick one, and then write a witty, brief commentary on the event. For specific methodology of picking a topic, look here.


I also use this blog for short-form or journalistic writing examples. Feel free to reach out regarding commissioned writing or collaborations.

Notes

Writing is one of my favorite things to do, so working on this project is really no chore. My true passion is for fiction and longer form writing, but this blog is just like a short daily workout. I don't take it too seriously.


I am attempting to get some other friends to also write on this blog to help bulk up the content!
